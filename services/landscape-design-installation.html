  • Sod, seeding & soil prep

Why it matters

Good landscape design reads as one decision, not a pile of separate plants. We work in curved, deliberate lines — boulders and grasses set with real spacing, hostas and perennials placed to fill in rather than crowd — so the bed looks designed the day it goes in, not just planted.
