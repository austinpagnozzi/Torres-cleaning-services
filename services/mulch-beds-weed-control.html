Why it matters

Of everything on this list, fresh mulch and a sharp bed edge move the needle on curb appeal the fastest — often in a single visit. We cut a clean edge against the lawn, hand-weed before we lay anything down, and use a pre-emergent so the bed still looks like this in July, not just the day we leave.
